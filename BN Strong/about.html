About BN Strong

Engineered for Excellence. Showing you what others don't.

Our Philosophy

A Complete Design-Build Powerhouse

BN Strong Engineering Group was founded on a simple premise: to eliminate the friction between architectural concept and structural reality. We are a unified Design-Build Engineering Company operating across Kenya, bringing precision and scale to every deployment.

Too often, projects suffer from fragmented communication between designers, builders, and specialist contractors. By uniting our four specialized divisions—BN Aluminium, BN Shades, BN Designs Build, and Site Work & Engineering—under one roof, we guarantee seamless execution.

We pride ourselves on absolute transparency. From the initial groundbreaking to the final installation of premium European-series aluminium setups, our clients have full visibility into our rigorous process.
