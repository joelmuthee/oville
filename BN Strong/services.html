Our Core Divisions

Four highly specialized engineering teams perfectly synchronized to deliver complete design-build installations.

Division 01

BN Aluminium

Premium European Series aluminium solutions built for longevity.

BN Aluminium specializes in the precision fabrication and installation of modern aluminium windows, heavy-duty doors, and custom balcony balustrades. By strictly relying on European profiles, we eliminate the flimsiness associated with budget installations.

  • Sleek Modern Finishes: Minimalistic frames maximizing glass surface areas.
  • High-Security: Embedded heavy-duty, multi-point locking systems.
  • Precision Fabricated Wraps: Aluminium cladding customized to exact property dimensions.

Division 02

BN Shades

Transforming challenging outdoor spaces into sanctuaries of style and serenity.

Whether it is sheltering your vehicles or creating an outdoor dining haven, BN Shades executes installations with rigorous exactness. We design for the severe Kenyan sun while prioritizing an elegant, chic profile.

  • Waterproof Shade Nets: Form-fitting carports and patio covers.
  • Rooftop Gazebos: Sleek aluminium and glass structures for high-end residential spaces.
  • Outdoor Life & Protection: UV-resistant materials designed to withstand harsh elements.

Division 03

BN Designs Build

Full architectural design and construction management.

Because BN Strong is fundamentally a Design-Build company, this division is the central nervous system of our operations. We translate your concept into structural reality, eliminating communication misfires between independent architects and generic on-site contractors.

  • Architectural Consultation: Translating modern aesthetics into viable blueprints.
  • Construction Management: Direct oversight from groundbreaking to final handover.
  • End-to-End Progression: A totally streamlined operation ensuring time and budget compliance.

Division 04

Site Work & Engineering

Laying the robust physical groundwork for architectural success.

We show what others don't, emphasizing transparency where it matters most: the framing, steel framework, and bare-ground site prep. By guaranteeing the integrity of our foundational structures, everything placed upon them remains secure indefinitely.

  • Structural Fabrication: Heavy truss fabrication and rigid steel framing.
  • Site Preparation: Excavation and leveling tailored to specific design requirements.
  • Protective Engineering: Severe anti-rust treatments and professional painting for all structural elements.
