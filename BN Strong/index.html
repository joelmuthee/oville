Who We Are

Engineered For Excellence

At BN Strong Engineering Group, our core philosophy is simple: we show you what others don't. We believe in absolute transparency throughout our entire process, demonstrating the journey from a bare patch of ground to a stunning final installation.

As a comprehensive Design-Build Engineering Company based in Kenya, we offer end-to-end solutions that guarantee modern aesthetics, strict structural integrity, and premium security.

Our work spans across architectural design, robust structural fabrication, premium European-series aluminium setups, and chic outdoor shade environments. Your project is our priority.
