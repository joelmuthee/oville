About Us

Integrity. Innovation. Excellence.

Our Story

A Legacy of Excellence

Founded in 2015 by Eng. Paul Karara Ngunjiri, Oville Associates was established with a singular mission: to provide innovative, cost-effective, and sustainable engineering solutions to the East African market.

Over the years, we have grown from a small consultancy to a powerhouse in Civil and Structural Engineering, trusted by major corporations and government bodies alike. Our approach is rooted in a deep understanding of local challenges combined with global best practices.

Our Values

Oville Philosophy

Work Ethic

At Oville Associates, we have a moral code that includes the obligation to carry out work that is simply well done. Our output must be without blemish, must be done to completion and with due care to finer details. We seek to provide ordinary services in extra ordinary ways.

Technology

As Engineers, technology is at the heart of our practice. At Oville Associates, we embrace and invest in the most current technologies to enable us serve our clients most efficiently. Use of technology enables us to deliver designs on time, communicate efficiently and keep our records secure and easily accessible. All our data is securely stored on a cloud platform

Clients

Our clients are our first priority. Our work is invariably aligned to the aspirations of our clients for their projects.

Staff

We value our staff as much as we value the business they assist us to handle. All our staff are well trained in their areas of specialisation and in interpersonal skills to make our clients’ interaction with Oville Associates as pleasant as possible.

Continuous Professional Development

We appreciate that the ethical requirement of working well necessarily entails the obligation, also ethical, of continuous professional development, even more necessary today because of the accelerated advance of science and technology. At Oville Associates, we are very open to new ideas and are continuously engaging professional development to broaden our knowledge on new and best practises in the industry to enable us serve our clients better.

Meet The Team

Eng. Paul Karara Ngunjiri

CE, MIEK, MAAK(E)
Principal Engineer

Eng. Paul Karara Ngunjiri holds a Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering 2nd Class Honours Upper division from Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology. He is registered with Engineers Board of Kenya (EBK) as a Consulting Engineer and is a Corporate Member of the Institution of Engineers of Kenya (IEK). He is also a member of the Architectural Association of Kenya – Engineers Chapter (AAK-E).

Eng. Karara has vast experience in design and construction supervision of buildings and associated infrastructure in Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda and South Sudan. Among the projects he has been involved in are high-rise office blocks, apartment blocks, residential estates, learning institutions, hospitals and hotels etc. Eng. Karara is responsible all technical work at Oville Associates and is also responsible for the day to day running of Oville Associates.

Eng. Brian Kuria

PE, MIEK
Associate Director, Water

Eng. Brian Kuria holds a Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering 2nd Class Honours Upper division from Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology. He is registered with Engineers Board of Kenya (EBK) as a professional Engineer and is a Corporate Member of the Institution of Engineers of Kenya (IEK).

Eng. Kuria is an impact biased Engineer and accomplished project manager with over 12-years’ experience in the development sector. He has successfully developed, designed, and coordinated implementation of programmes and projects funded by various bilateral and multilateral development partners such as The World Bank, African Development Bank, DANIDA, USAID and French Development Agency. He has particular experience in investment planning, institutional strengthening, water supply & sanitation systems design, construction supervision, technical audits and overall project management. His experience spans 6 countries in the East and Southern Africa region.
