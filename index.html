Who We Are

Engineering Excellence Tailored to Your Needs

Oville Associates is a leading Civil and Structural Engineering firm dedicated to delivering high-quality, sustainable solutions for the built environment. As a proud member of the Kenya Green Building Society (KGBS), we champion environmentally responsible design.

We actively partner with international developers and engineering firms from Europe, America, and beyond, providing local expertise for global projects. Our operations are powered by secure cloud technology, ensuring 24/7 data accessibility and client information security.

From complex structural designs to forensic investigations, our team ensures every project meets the highest standards of safety and efficiency.
